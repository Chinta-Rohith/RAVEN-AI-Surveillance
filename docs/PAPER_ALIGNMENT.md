# DeTrAck Paper Alignment & Compliance Matrix

This document provides a formal comparison between the research paper:

> **"DeTrAck: UAV Detection and Tracking Using Neural Networks Ensemble on Surveillance Systems"**  
> *Trandafir-Liviu Serghei and Dan Popescu*  
> Published in *IEEE Access*, December 2025.

and the **RAVEN** (Real-time Agentic Vision for UAV Navigation) implementation.

---

## Architectural Alignment Matrix

| Paper Component | Paper Specification | RAVEN Implementation | Status | Classification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Detector** | YOLO11 (YOLO11s selected for optimal precision-speed trade-off) | `backend/detection/yolo_detector.py` using Ultralytics YOLO11 with configurable confidence & IoU thresholds | **Implemented** | Core Paper |

| **False-Positive Suppression** | 2.6× reduction in false positives via VaL_CNN candidate verification | In-loop ROI patch extraction and validation prior to tracking admission | **Implemented** | Core Paper |

## Research Integrity & Disclaimer

* **Authentic Models**: RAVEN does not fabricate or mock accuracy numbers, mAP, or FPS.
* **Weight Transparency**: If custom fine-tuned weights are not present on disk, the system explicitly reports:
  `"VaL_CNN weights not found. Please train the validation model."`
* **Metric Labeling**: Any experimental metrics generated by RAVEN are strictly labeled as **"RAVEN reproduction result"** and never falsely attributed to the published DeTrAck paper results.
* **Non-Lethal Academic Tool**: RAVEN is strictly an academic research and situational-awareness prototype with no offensive fire-control or kinetic mechanisms.
